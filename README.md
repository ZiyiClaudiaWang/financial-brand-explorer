# Financial Brand Explorer

An interactive dashboard for exploring how financial-services brand awareness, usage, and preference differ across generations

**[Open the interactive dashboard](https://ziyiclaudiawang.github.io/financial-brand-explorer/)**

## What you can explore

- **Brand leaderboard** — rank 30 brands by awareness, current use, most preferred, least preferred, or net preference; compare overall results or individual generations
- **Preference & usage map** — see how reported current use and most-preferred selections relate across brands
- **Brand comparison** — compare up to four brands across Gen Z, Millennials, Gen X, and Baby Boomers
- **Generational heatmap** — scan brand-by-generation differences and sort by metric or generational gap
- **Brand details** — view a brand's results, within-survey rank, and differences across generations

## Data & interpretation

The dashboard uses a **cleaned, unweighted survey sample of 982 respondents** across 30 financial-services brands and four generations

| Measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Awareness | Percentage who recognize the brand |
| Current use | Percentage who report currently using the brand |
| Most preferred | Percentage selecting the brand among their most-wanted brands |
| Least preferred | Percentage selecting the brand among their least-wanted brands |
| Net preference | Most preferred minus least preferred, in percentage points (pp) |

Results describe **this survey sample**, not population market shares or causal effects. Preference and usage are separate measures, not stages of a conversion funnel

## Project files

- `index.html` — interactive, browser-based dashboard
- `data/brand_metrics.json` — aggregated brand-by-generation metrics
- `assets/logos/` — brand logos used in the dashboard

The dashboard is a static site published with **GitHub Pages**, with no backend or account required. Open the link above to explore it; updates to the published `main` branch are reflected on the site after deployment
